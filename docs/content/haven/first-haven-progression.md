# First Haven Progression

**Status:** Accepted vertical-slice progression package  
**Last updated:** 2026-07-19  
**Related:** [Haven Buildings](../../systems/haven-buildings.md), [Economy](../../systems/economy.md), [Progression](../../systems/progression.md), [The Unlit Road](../expeditions/the-unlit-road.md)

## Design goal

The first successful boss Return creates a genuine Haven choice: the player can solve one immediate problem, not every problem. Wick gates the timing of major construction; Timber and Stone remain useful as construction, repair, and Forge materials rather than becoming decorative surplus.

## First-clear material contract

| Source | Guaranteed Haven materials |
|---|---|
| Each standard combat | 1 Timber + 1 Stone |
| Lantern-Smother victory | 4 Timber + 4 Stone + 1 Wick |

The minimum three-standard-combat route therefore returns with **7 Timber, 7 Stone, and 1 Wick** before optional Event rewards. This guarantees one starting core building, but not two.

Combat-forward routes accumulate modest Timber/Stone surplus for a future project, basic-vessel commissioning, Forge repair, and later building upgrades. Wick remains deliberately scarce and arrives through major victories or rare world discoveries.

## Buildings

| Building | Construction | Tier-1 function |
|---|---:|---|
| Pillarhouse | Built at founding | Pillars, Haven Gloom, memorial, Ember-Shard relight rite, embark risk display. No slice upgrade. |
| Cinder Forge | 7 Timber, 5 Stone, 1 Wick | Shared armory, basic vessel commission, Safe Imprint. |
| The Quiet House | 5 Timber, 7 Stone, 1 Wick | One free injury treatment on construction; later ordinary treatment. |
| The Wardyard | 6 Timber, 6 Stone, 1 Wick | Records earned Leadership Points as permanent survivor attributes. |

The first boss awards the Ember Vault blueprint; the second awards The Wayfarer blueprint. Both are blueprint-only in the vertical slice.

## Cinder Forge

The Forge is the Haven's shared armory and experimental workshop. All returned gear is presented and prepared here, but the Forge never creates an inventory cap or withholds player-owned equipment from ordinary equipping.

| Action | Inputs | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Armory | None | Inspect, compare, equip, and commit returned gear to an expedition. |
| Commission Vessel | 3 Timber, 2 Stone, 1 Emberglass | Choose a known basic vessel at Salvaged quality. It is reliable starting equipment, never best-in-slot. |
| Safe Imprint | Returned gear + unlearned scroll + Emberglass | Use the accepted Safe Imprint recipe and disclosed odds. |

### Deferred Forge blueprint: Sacrificial Reforge

Sacrificial Reforge is a future Forge blueprint, not a tier-1 action. It will consume two or more gear items plus Haven materials and Emberglass to make a stronger targeted item.

Its disclosed possible costs are intentionally Haven-facing:

- a named survivor begins their next expedition **Scorched**, reducing starting HP for that expedition; or
- the Forge becomes **Damaged** and its special craft actions require Timber and Stone to repair.

Stored gear is never destroyed by a Forge mishap. Rare/Legendary-grade targeted forging is also future content.

## Quiet House

- Building it includes one free treatment of Injured, Wounded, or Drained.
- Later ordinary treatment costs **2 Emberglass** per injury.
- Ember Shards are not routine medical currency. They remain reserved for pillar repair and later premium projects.

## Leadership growth and the Wardyard

Every expedition begins at **Expedition Level 1**, regardless of a hero's permanent Haven growth.

1. During an expedition, a hero can earn one temporary level and assign one temporary point to VIT, DEX, STR, or INT. It takes effect immediately for that run.
2. On a wipe, temporary levels and temporary points vanish with the party.
3. On a successful boss-clear Return, each surviving expedition hero earns one pending **Leadership Point**.
4. If the Wardyard exists, the player immediately assigns that point permanently to one attribute of that survivor. The permanent choice need not match the temporary point used during the run.
5. Pending Leadership Points wait only until the Wardyard exists. Building the Wardyard immediately requires the player to make each permanent, no-respec choice.

The vertical slice caps this at **one Leadership Point per surviving hero per expedition**. It produces gradual permanent leaders without making the starting kits overpower the Band-1 baseline. Future Wardyard tiers may alter this cap, unlock drills, or introduce subclasses.

## Post-return decision rule

There is no artificial one-action limit. The first-clear material budget and Wick bottleneck naturally restrict the player to one core construction. Once a building exists, its operations consume their own specific inputs; the player chooses whether to preserve materials for the next structure, care for a survivor, or invest in equipment.
