# Vanguard

**Status:** Draft (name locked for must-ship)  
**Type:** Class  
**Last updated:** 2026-07-16

## Identity

- **ID:** `vanguard`
- **One-line fantasy:** The wall — grim defenders who hold the lantern line so others can strike.
- **Role:** Frontline tank / protector
- **Starting schools:** `iron` + `bastion`

## Player fantasy

You spend AP on blocks, taunts, and lantern wards; your deck is heavy with Iron/Bastion ability and attack cards that keep allies alive. Subclass lets you branch into fire-ward or Gloom-bargain kits.

## Mechanics summary

- High VIT / block-oriented starter deck (Bastion + Iron)
- Abilities: Brace, Guard, Lantern Beacon–style wards (as cards)
- Can learn Ember or Umbra only after subclass
- Synergizes with offhand shields that inject Bastion Block cards
