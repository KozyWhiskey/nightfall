## Identity

- **ID:** `shadowblade`
- **One-line fantasy:** Pragmatic survivors who fight darkness by becoming part of it.
- **Role:** Melee DPS / initiative predator
- **Starting schools:** `veil` + `iron`

## Player fantasy

High DEX, act early on the timeline, chain Iron attacks, use Veil Mist/Dodge abilities from kit and gear. Spells are spice until subclass opens Umbra or Storm for hybrid nasty.

## Mechanics summary

- Attack-heavy deck (Iron); ability cards for mobility/evasion (Veil)
- DEX feeds initiative (combat fantasy centerpiece)
- Subclasses split burst assassin (Umbra) vs skirmisher (Storm) — third school opens the spell/ability learn pool
