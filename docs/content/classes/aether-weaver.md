## Identity

- **ID:** `aether_weaver`
- **One-line fantasy:** Reckless scholars wielding the chaotic Weave left after Nightfall.
- **Role:** Ranged magical DPS / spellcraft poster child
- **Starting schools:** `aether` + `ember`

## Player fantasy

Your deck leans spells; you chase fusion greed, soft/hard tradeoffs, and **cross-school reactions**. Attacks are few but gear can inject cantrips. Subclass opens Tide, Storm, or deeper control — where Water+Lightning stun hybrids live.

## Mechanics summary

- High INT / mana pool
- Learn gate: Aether + Ember at start; third school from subclass
- Spellcraft risk ladder is *your* playground; cross-school reactions are a signature fantasy
- Fragile VIT — positioning via initiative and ally guards
