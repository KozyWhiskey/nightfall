# Event: The Last Courier

**Status:** Draft  
**Event ID:** `last_courier`  
**Region tags:** `frontier`, `survivor`, `social_horror`  
**Last updated:** 2026-07-18

**Build 1 resolution authority:** [Vertical-Slice Tuning](../expeditions/vertical-slice-tuning.md) supplies exact costs, Gloom changes, and delayed results.

## Setup

A wounded courier lies beside a failing hand-lantern, one hand closed around a sealed route ledger. They insist there are still people waiting farther down the old road. The ledger is warm with names that have not yet been crossed out.

## Choices

| Choice | Cost / requirement | Outcome | Risk |
|--------|--------------------|---------|------|
| Escort them to the next safe point | Spend Rations; take an `escort` burden | On successful Return, gain a Wayfarer contact / future recruit thread | Next Rest is weaker; may trigger an ambush |
| Take the ledger and leave | None | Gain a settlement/map clue with future discovery value | Gloom rises; record an abandonment flag |
| Feed the lantern | Spend Emberglass or an Ember Shard | Reduce run Gloom; gain a small ward for the next combat | Loses the recruit/map opportunity |

## Mechanical effects

- The event establishes that people are a valuable expedition reward, not only a resource source.
- `escort` is a clear temporary expedition flag; it must show its current burden and promised Return reward in the UI.
- Exact material/Gloom amounts are in the tuning table.

## Co-op notes

Embark leader confirms. Escort, map clue, and Gloom outcomes belong to the host Haven's expedition state.

## Acceptance criteria

- Every choice serves a distinct player motive: people, knowledge, or immediate safety.
- The abandonment consequence is legible but not moralizing copy.
- The courier is written as an adult survivor with agency, not a child-rescue trope.
