# Event: The Choir in the Bark

**Status:** Draft  
**Event ID:** `choir_in_the_bark`  
**Region tags:** `frontier`, `memory_horror`, `gloom`, `craft`  
**Last updated:** 2026-07-18

**Build 1 resolution authority:** [Vertical-Slice Tuning](../expeditions/vertical-slice-tuning.md) supplies exact costs, probabilities, and results.

## Setup

The black-barked trees speak names from the Haven. Some belong to the dead. Some belong to people who are still there. Beneath the voices, resin runs like dark glass through wounds in the wood.

## Choices

| Choice | Cost / requirement | Outcome | Risk |
|--------|--------------------|---------|------|
| Carve the names free | Spend time / accept a small burden | Reduce Gloom; gain a protective memory charm | A hero may gain temporary Strain |
| Follow a familiar voice | None | Chance at a rare scroll, relic, or settlement clue | Ambush or Gloom spike |
| Cut the black resin | None | Gain a powerful craft component | Result carries a curse or instability tag |

## Mechanical effects

- This is the vertical slice's clearest memory-horror event.
- The resin must be useful enough that taking the curse is a real greed decision.
- The charm is a small one-run protective effect, not a permanent power reward.

## Co-op notes

Embark leader confirms. If a voice references a player-owned hero, all party members can read the same event text; do not hide critical choice information.

## Acceptance criteria

- The event feels unsettling through recognition and loss, not a jump scare.
- The cursed craft component creates a valid build decision rather than an obvious trap.
