# First Scroll Pool

**Status:** Accepted vertical-slice content seed  
**Last updated:** 2026-07-18  
**Related:** [Spell and Ability Framework](../../systems/spell-and-ability-framework.md), [Balance Reference](../../systems/balance-reference.md), [Vertical-Slice Rewards](../expeditions/vertical-slice-rewards.md)

## Scope

These ten scroll patterns establish early build direction without replacing the basic starter kits. Eight are immediately learnable by the starting Vanguard/Aether Weaver party. Umbra patterns are valuable physical scrolls, craft inputs, and Haven discoveries, but are not learnable until the Umbra school is discovered in later content.

Every found scroll is a physical item. Learning consumes it. A player may instead preserve it, craft with it, or return it to Haven under the existing spellcraft rules.

## Pool

| School | Scroll | Rarity | Cost | Effect | Build invitation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Iron | `sundering_stroke` — Sundering Stroke | Imbued | 1 AP + 1 Stamina | Deal 5 physical damage and remove 6 Block from the target. | Martial answer to defensive enemies. |
| Iron | `crack_open` — Crack Open | Rare | 1 AP + 2 Stamina | Deal 8 physical damage. If the target is Exposed, deal 3 additional damage. | Rewards Exposed synergy. |
| Bastion | `still_wall` — Still Wall | Salvaged | 1 AP + 1 Stamina | Gain 9 Block. The first enemy whose next damaging intent is fully absorbed by this Block becomes Weakened for 1 turn. | Rewards reading intent and proactive defense. |

| Aether | `aether_needle` — Aether Needle | Salvaged | 1 AP + 1 Mana | Deal 6 Aether damage. This damage ignores Block. | Precision answer to guarded enemies. |

| Ember | `kindle_wound` — Kindle Wound | Salvaged | 1 AP + 1 Mana | Deal 3 Ember damage and apply 2 Burn. | Burn-focused pressure. |

## Intentional distinctions

- Iron breaks defense through force; Aether bypasses or unravels it.
- Bastion converts well-timed defense into protection or Weakened pressure rather than burst damage.
- Ember owns the first all-enemy spell, but its 3-Mana cost prevents routine use.
- Umbra establishes timing/setup identity rather than becoming generic dark damage.
- No scroll is an automatic upgrade. Every one can be learned now, preserved for a future hero, used in crafting, or brought home as Haven knowledge.

## First-boss constraint

The Lantern-Smother’s initial three-item reward choice remains **gear only**. A later expansion may add a Rare scroll option to boss reward pools after ordinary-combat scroll value is proven in playtests.

## Content constraints

- Do not add another Draw or Retain scroll to this first pool.
- Do not add routine Stun, Poison, summon, resource-restoration, or permanent-aura cards.
- Effects must use the shared declarative card/effect grammar and be readable in one combat tooltip.
