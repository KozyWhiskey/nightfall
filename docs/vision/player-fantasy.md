# Player Fantasy

**Status:** Locked  
**Last updated:** 2026-07-16

## Who the player is

The player is a **survivor leading survivors** — expedition leaders leaving a fragile Haven to gather what the settlement needs: resources, relics, knowledge, and people.

In solo play they command the whole party. In co-op, friends share the expedition — lobby assigns which heroes each human owns (a friend may pilot more than one).

## Fantasy layers

### The expedition

- Travel a shattered, Gloom-touched world.
- Choose dangerous paths for better rewards.
- Build a kit so unique it feels like *this* party’s story.
- Pull off combat combos that clear the field or make allies untouchable.

### The roster

- Heroes are people, not loadouts. You level them, subclass them, gear them.
- You do not expect everyone to live forever — but you fight for every life (Darkest Dungeon attachment).
- Death hurts the Haven and the player’s plans, not just the run score.

### The Haven

- Each account has a **named Haven** — the place expeditions start from and return to.
- Buildings you choose to raise first define *your* Haven’s identity.
- When the last light fails, the town can die. Scars remain. You begin again.

## Party fantasy

Default party size: **2–3**. Small enough to read every turn; large enough for roles (tank, striker, support/weaver).

Recruits, tavern strangers, and rescued survivors feed the roster fantasy: not every face is permanent, but some become legends of the Haven.

## Tone of the hero

Not invincible champions. **Desperate competence** — lantern-bearers and grit-holders carving a path so others can sleep one more night.
